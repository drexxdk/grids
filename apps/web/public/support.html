Support and data deletion

Need help with Gridz? Contact incrementish@gmail.com. Never send passwords, client secrets, session cookies or sign-in codes.

Delete your account and cloud data

  1. Open Gridz and choose Log in in the header.
  2. Sign in using a provider connected to the Gridz account you want to delete, then choose user icon → Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and server data in Account removal, then confirm.

This deletes your live Gridz account, all connected sign-in identities, active sessions, saved grids and private/published presets, including data brought in by confirmed account merges. If you have separate accounts that you have not connected or merged, repeat for each account you want removed. You can instead delete individual items or make a published preset private. Copies of a published preset already saved by others are unaffected. See the Privacy Policy for recovery-backup retention.

Remove data from this browser

Account deletion and sign-out intentionally keep local drafts and uploads. To remove them, use your browser's site-data settings to clear storage for gritz.pages.dev. This also removes local images and the offline cache, and cannot be undone. Installed apps may have separate storage; clear that too if applicable.

Disconnect Discord or another provider

To remove a sign-in connection within Gridz, open user icon → Account → Connected sign-in providers → Disconnect and confirm. Sessions signed in through that provider are revoked, but saves and local uploads are kept. Keep at least one available sign-in provider connected. If you disconnect the provider used for the current session, sign in again using a remaining provider.

To revoke the external authorization too, in Discord open User Settings → Authorized Apps → Gridz → Deauthorize. Other providers offer similar connected-app settings. Deauthorization does not itself disconnect the identity in Gridz or delete your account; use the Gridz actions above as needed.

Connect providers or combine existing accounts

Sign in to the Gridz account you want to keep, then choose user icon → Account → Connected sign-in providers → Connect and authorize the other provider. If it already has a Gridz account, review the account and save counts, then confirm the merge within 10 minutes or cancel. All incoming grids and presets are preserved, including duplicate names and visibility. Your current account name is kept and sessions from the incoming account are signed out. A merge cannot be undone automatically. One identity per provider is supported; resolve conflicting connections before merging. Browser drafts and uploads are not changed.

Cannot sign in, or need a data copy?

Email incrementish@gmail.com with the provider used and your request (for example, account deletion, access, correction or a copy of saved data). We will help verify account ownership before disclosing or deleting data. Do not send your provider password or tokens. You can also contact us about removal of data from recovery backups.

Report content or a problem

For an inappropriate shared preset, copyright/privacy concern or technical problem, include the preset name or ID if available, what happened, and enough non-sensitive detail to investigate. Do not attach private images or someone else's sensitive information. For security reports, email privately rather than posting exploit details publicly.

About Gridz

Gridz lets you create and export image grids. Provider login is optional, image files stay out of our cloud database, and no Discord bot or server installation is needed.
