Support and data deletion

Need help with Gridz? Contact incrementish@gmail.com. Never send passwords, client secrets, session cookies or sign-in codes.

Delete your account and cloud data

  1. Open Gridz and choose Account and saved grids (the cloud icon).
  2. Sign in using the same Google, Facebook or Discord account you used to save your work.
  3. Choose Delete account and server data in Account removal, then confirm.

This deletes your live Gridz account, active sessions, saved grids and private/published presets. Different providers create separate accounts; repeat for each account you want removed. You can instead delete individual items or make a published preset private. Copies of a published preset already saved by others are unaffected. See the Privacy Policy for recovery-backup retention.

Remove data from this browser

Account deletion and sign-out intentionally keep local drafts and uploads. To remove them, use your browser's site-data settings to clear storage for gritz.pages.dev. This also removes local images and the offline cache, and cannot be undone. Installed apps may have separate storage; clear that too if applicable.

Disconnect Discord or another provider

In Discord, open User Settings → Authorized Apps → Gridz → Deauthorize. Other providers offer similar connected-app settings. This stops the provider authorization but does not itself delete your Gridz account; use the deletion steps above as well.

Cannot sign in, or need a data copy?

Email incrementish@gmail.com with the provider used and your request (for example, account deletion, access, correction or a copy of saved data). We will help verify account ownership before disclosing or deleting data. Do not send your provider password or tokens. You can also contact us about removal of data from recovery backups.

Report content or a problem

For an inappropriate shared preset, copyright/privacy concern or technical problem, include the preset name or ID if available, what happened, and enough non-sensitive detail to investigate. Do not attach private images or someone else's sensitive information. For security reports, email privately rather than posting exploit details publicly.

About Gridz

Gridz lets you create and export image grids. Provider login is optional, image files stay out of our cloud database, and no Discord bot or server installation is needed.
